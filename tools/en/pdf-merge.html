Merge PDFs without uploading them

Add your files, arrange the order, and download a single PDF. Pages are copied as they are rather than re-rendered, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution.

    How to merge PDFs

    1. Add the filesDrag PDFs onto the box or tap it to browse. Each file's page count appears as soon as it has been read.
    2. Set the orderUse the ↑ and ↓ buttons to move a document up or down. The merged file follows this list from top to bottom.
    3. Merge and downloadTap “Merge PDFs”, wait a moment, then download the result. The file is created in memory and disappears when you close the tab.

    Merging without a server

    Online PDF services usually upload your document, merge it on their machines and send it back. For contracts, payslips, medical records or ID copies that is an avoidable risk. This page loads the open-source pdf-lib library once and merges the pages in your browser, so the files never leave your device.

    What happens to quality and signatures

    Pages are copied page object by page object, not rasterised, so text stays selectable, links inside pages keep working and images keep their original resolution. Document-level bookmarks, form fields and digital signatures are not carried over — a signed PDF shows as unsigned after merging, which is expected because its bytes changed.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. The merge runs in your browser and the result is created in memory. Nothing is sent over the network.

    Can I reorder pages between files?

    You can reorder whole files with the arrow buttons; the page order inside each file stays as it was. To rearrange individual pages, split the PDF first.

    Does merging reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied rather than converted to images, so text stays crisp and selectable.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not while they are locked. Remove the password in a PDF reader, save an unprotected copy and add that here.

    How many files can I merge?

    There is no fixed number — the limit is your device's memory. Ten to twenty typical documents are no problem on a phone; hundreds of scanned pages are better on a computer.

    What about digital signatures?

    A merged document is a new file, so any signature becomes invalid. Keep the signed original if you need it for legal purposes.
